our game is advanced ping pong in this game we have two walls between the walls and two paddle between the paddles are a ball the ball bounces between the walls and you have to hit it with the paddle and stop the ball from hitting the wall you have. we have 3 maps: The Void, Gravity Well, Pulsar ,4 difficulty: chill, standard, overdrive, insane, 3 ball sizes: micro, classic, heavy, and 2 control options: independent[w/s + arrow keys], mirror link. when you start the game the paddles are controlled by w/s and arrow keys. you have 3 life's if you hit the wall thrice the game end. There is a alignment option when to align the paddles in a staright line you score extra points and if you hit 5 alignment you get a extra life. every 10 points a new mode comes. when you score 10 points the controls switches like the left paddle is controlled by arrow keys and the right paddle is controlled by w/s. when you score 20 points the screen blacks out and only the paddles and the balls trail is visible. if you somehow score 30 points one more ball is added and the game becomes very difficult. there is also a randomize button where if you click it the game automatically selects modes for you and starts the game you can also select modes manually. on the homescreen you can see the highest score and the previous game scores as well.
